# About

I'm a Software Engineer at Tesla's Digital Experience organization, where I build scalable, containerized web applications that enhance the lives of Tesla customers and owners worldwide. My work sits at the intersection of cutting-edge web technology, AI and human-centered design.
I earned my Master's in Computer Science from the University of Florida, specializing in Intelligent Systems with a research focus on Deep Learning, Generative AI, and Computer Vision. During my graduate studies, I served as an Application Developer with the <a href="https://ceecs.education.ufl.edu/">Anita Zucker Center for Childhood Excellence</a>, where I developed <a href="https://enact.rc.ufl.edu/">ENACT</a>, a geo-fencing mobile application cal designed to foster Rich Language Behaviors between parents and young children—leveraging technology to make a meaningful impact in early childhood development.
Previously, I led frontend development for Oracle's <a href="https://www.oracle.com/construction-engineering/construction-intelligence-cloud/">Construction Intelligence Cloud</a> as a Software Engineer, spearheading the CIC Admin App. My diverse technical foundation was built through internships at <a href="https://www.sc.com/">Standard Chartered Bank</a> and <a href="https://forty4hz.com/">Forty4Hz</a>, spanning fintech, enterprise software, and emerging technologies.
I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Manipal Institute of Technology and am driven by transforming ideas into real-world impact. This philosophy materialized during the pandemic when I launched <a href="https://www.getmyvaccine.in">getmyvaccine.in</a>, a platform that helped over 10,000 people secure COVID vaccine appointments during India's critical vaccination drive.
Beyond engineering, I'm captivated by the frontiers of human knowledge—from sci-fi literature to research in consciousness and quantum mechanics. I draw inspiration from visionaries like Roger Penrose, Nikola Tesla, Elon Musk, and Mahatma Gandhi, who each redefined possibilities in their fields while advancing human progress.
